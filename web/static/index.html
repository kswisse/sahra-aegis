Research Validation Summary

A controlled 270-run study (Malik & Qureshi 2026, doi:10.5281/zenodo.22957164) demonstrated that LLM-orchestrated multi-agent systems suffered coordination breakdown (74.44% task success), whereas deterministic orchestration recovered 93.33% reliability without inference costs or hallucinated safety overrides.

Why SAHRA AEGIS is 100% Deterministic

In mission-critical life-safety applications such as occupational heat-stress monitoring in the United Arab Emirates, non-deterministic language models introduce latency, hallucination risks, and coordination failures.

SAHRA's core control plane is implemented as a strictly typed state machine (src/sahra/kernel.py). An LLM is permitted exclusively in external communication channels for phrasing natural language advisories, never in the decision or gating path.

0% Silent Downgrade

Consequential actions (such as worker evacuation or activity escalation) cannot be silently downgraded or dropped. If no authorized approver is assigned, the action is refused and held in the HITL queue.

Conservative-Max Arbiter

When localized IoT weather stations and satellite/grid weather tiers disagree by more than 1.0°C, the Arbiter Agent rules the higher value to ensure worker protection.

Append-Only Audit Log

All gate decisions, verifications, and approvals are written sequentially to a JSONL log file, creating a tamper-resistant evidence chain for regulatory compliance.
